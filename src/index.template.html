A crate over a pulley

Set the crate’s weight, or slide the anchor on the ground to change where the rope runs. The pulley cannot change the rope’s tension — only its direction.

Equilibrium

A frictionless pulley redirects a rope without changing its tension, so both segments of the cable carry the crate’s weight. Two equal pulls add to a resultant along the bisector of the angle between them — which is why the support rope always sits at θ = 45° + β/2, and why loading the crate more heavily never moves it.
